About Lenovo

Lenovo is a leading global technology powerhouse, best known as the world's largest personal computer (PC) manufacturer by market share.

Founded: Established on November 1, 1984, in Beijing, China, originally under the name Legend.

Key Historical Milestones
+ 2005: Acquired IBM personal computer division, including the iconic ThinkPad laptop line, transforming Lenovo into a major international player.
+ 2014: Completed the acquisition of Motorola Mobility from Google and IBMs x86 server business, significantly expanding its footprint in smartphones and enterprise data centers.
